# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free contribution experience.

Be considerate in discussion, assume good intent while addressing impact, and keep criticism focused on code and ideas. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and threats are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct undermines a safe and constructive community. For concerns, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub.
